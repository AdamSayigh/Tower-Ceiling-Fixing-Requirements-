01The Requirement
Solid behind|the lining

The door's hangers and motor fix up through the ceiling lining at set positions. On a truss roof the lining is usually fixed to battens, which leaves a cavity between the trusses and the lining. At every pad that cavity is filled with solid LVL (or as the engineer specifies), hard against the back of the lining, over the full area of the pad.

Door
Behind fit · up to 3100 high · for other door types or heights, Tower supplies the setout
Ceiling
Roof trusses · lining fixed to ceiling battens or furring channels. Where the lining is fixed direct to the trusses, the noggings finish flush with the underside of the bottom chords. Suspended ceilings: contact Tower.
Noggings

Engineer
The truss designer confirms the trusses for the door loads; the truss designer or the builder's engineer details the nogging members and fixings. Pad positions, sizes and the no-cavity rule stay as drawn. Door weight from Tower, ideally before the trusses are ordered.
Stick-framed
Where the ceiling is framed with joists, Tower's stick-framed roof document applies. Setout and pad sizes are identical.

03Nogging Detail
Hard against|the lining

Where the lining hangs off battens, there is a cavity behind it. At every pad, LVL noggings fixed to the trusses fill that cavity, flush with the underside of the battens, so the lining sits hard against solid timber across the whole pad: no cavity, nothing to crush, nothing to pack.

What we check
Position
Every pad set out from the back of the opening, its edges and its centreline, as scheduled.
Size
Every pad at least its scheduled size, solid LVL (or as the engineer specifies) across its full area.
Flush
Every nogging flush with the underside of the battens, checked with a straightedge, so the lining sits hard against it. No gap, no step, no loose packers.
Structure
Truss designer's sign-off for the door loads and the nogging detail.
Why it matters
Our installers fix the door's hangers and motor through the lining into these pads, its tracks into the side room and its springs into the head. If the solid fixing is not there, not flush, or not where it is drawn, the door cannot be installed to our standard, and remedial work normally means opening up the finished ceiling or walls.
Tower's review is a check, not an acceptance of the framing. Compliance with this document remains with the builder.
